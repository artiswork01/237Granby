Granby Evidence Site
====================

Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your web host, keeping:
- index.html at the site root
- the media folder beside index.html

Reserved items:
- Items 81–86 remain visible placeholders in index.html.
- To add them later, put the media files in /media and replace the matching
  placeholder section in index.html.

Important:
- Three very large source videos (Items 33, 71, and 76) were intentionally excluded.
- Videos included here are compressed viewing copies for online presentation.
- Keep the original source files separately and unchanged.
